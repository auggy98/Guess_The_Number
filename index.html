💡 Hint: Need a strategy for 10 questions?
At first, it seems like removing the interactivity would require more questions, but it doesn't. One way to solve part two is to imagine my number written in binary. A number between 1 and 1,000 can be represented with 10 binary digits (the largest is 1111111111, which equals 1,023). So you can ask directly about the number itself. You start with "Is the 1st digit of your number in binary a 1?" After 10 questions, you will have described a specific 10-digit binary number!
